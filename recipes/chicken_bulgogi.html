Skillet Chicken Bulgogi

Home

Description

Korean inspired stir-fry featuring thinly sliced chicken marinated in a sweet, savoury and nutty soy-garlic glaze with toasted sesame seeds and touch of heat

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup chopped onion
  • 5 teaspoons soy sauce
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons minced garlic
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon of cayenne
  • Salt and ground black pepper to taste
  • 450 grams skinless, boneless chicken breasts cut into thin strips

Steps

  1. Whisk onion, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, sesame oil, sesame seeds, cayenne pepper, salt and black pepper together in a bowl and marinade until smooth
  2. Cook and stir chicken and marinade together in a large skillet over medium-high heat until chicken is cooked through (about 15 minutes)
